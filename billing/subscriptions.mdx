Flagmint bills **per organisation**, in EUR, with no per-seat fees. Manage the plan from **Settings → Billing** in the [dashboard](https://flagmint.com/dashboard). Only organisation **admins** can change billing.

Plan prices and feature limits are on [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans). How evaluations are counted is on [Usage](/billing/usage).

## Start on Free

New organisations start on **Free** unless a paid Stripe price is selected during onboarding. Free is €0 forever: 500 API calls/month (throttled, not blocked), 1 project, 5 flags.

To move to Pro or Growth, add a payment method and confirm the subscription in **Settings → Billing**. Custom plans are quoted by [sales@flagmint.com](mailto:sales@flagmint.com).

## Trial

Paid plans include a **14-day trial**. No second trial after you have used one (`has_used_paid_trial`). Remaining trial days are counted from organisation creation.

During a paid trial the organisation is `trialing`. When the trial ends without a successful payment, flags become **read-only** until you upgrade — evaluations against existing flags still run within the plan quota.

## Change plan

| Change | When it takes effect |
|--------|----------------------|
| Upgrade (Free → Pro, Pro → Growth, monthly → annual) | Immediately |
| Downgrade | End of the current billing period |
| Annual vs monthly | Annual saves ~17% (Pro €290/year, Growth €790/year) |

Upgrades and payment-method changes go through Stripe. You need a saved card before a paid subscription can be created.

## Cancel

Cancel from **Settings → Billing** (Stripe Customer Portal). The organisation stays on the paid plan until the period ends, then drops to **Free**. Flags and data are kept; features above Free limits are unavailable.

## Stripe Customer Portal

[`GET /billing/portal`](/api-reference/billing/get-billingportal) returns a short-lived Stripe URL. Admins use it to:

- Update payment methods
- View and download invoices
- Cancel or resume the subscription
- Update billing details

Do not store or reuse the portal URL.

Invoices are also listed at [`GET /billing/organizations/{orgId}/invoices`](/api-reference/billing/list-invoices-for-an-organization).

## Subscription status

| Status | Meaning | SDK evaluation |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| `trialing` | Paid trial in progress | Allowed |
| `active` | Paid plan in good standing | Allowed |
| `past_due` | Payment failed, Stripe retrying | Blocked (403) |
| `canceled` | Subscription cancelled | Blocked (403) |
| `incomplete` | First payment not confirmed | Blocked (403) |
| `incomplete_expired` | First payment window expired | Blocked (403) |

Inactive statuses apply to SSE, WebSocket, and REST evaluate. Fix billing in **Settings → Billing** (`/settings/subscription`) rather than rotating SDK keys.

Overage for Pro and Growth is added to the Stripe invoice at period close. See [Overage](/billing/plans#overage).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can change the plan?">
    Organisation admins only. Editors and viewers can use flags; they cannot open the billing portal or create a Stripe session.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where are invoices sent?">
    To the organisation `billing_email`, or the owner's email if that field is empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get another trial after cancelling?">
    No. A paid trial is once per organisation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if payment fails?">
    Stripe retries. The organisation becomes `past_due` and SDKs receive 403 until the invoice is paid or you return to Free after cancel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
